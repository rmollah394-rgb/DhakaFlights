Smart Tips to Fly Cheaper from Bangladesh

📅

Book 4–6 Weeks Ahead

International tickets from Dhaka (Dubai, Kuala Lumpur, Singapore) are usually cheapest 30–45 days before departure.

🛫

Compare Mid-Week Flights

Tuesdays and Wednesdays often have lower fares compared to Thursday or Saturday departures out of HSIA (DAC).

🧳

Check Baggage Included

Always compare total ticket cost including 20kg–30kg checked luggage before booking low-cost carriers.
